The software

Hopper is a desktop application for macOS that lets you transfer files over FTP, FTPS and SFTP, work with databases, use an SSH terminal and Git, and send HTTP requests. You can download and use the free version at no cost. Some features require a Hopper Pro license.

Licenses

A Hopper Pro license is a personal, non-transferable right to use Hopper Pro on up to two Macs at the same time. You can deactivate a Mac from the app to use the license on another one.

Each license includes one year of updates. After that year you keep using the last version released during it for as long as you want. Renewing gives you another year of updates.

Team licenses are counted per person. You may not share, resell or publish license keys.
