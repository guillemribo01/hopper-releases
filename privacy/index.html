Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

The app

Hopper runs on your Mac. Your connections, files, queries and requests stay on your computer and go only to the servers you connect to. Passwords are stored in the macOS Keychain.

Hopper has no analytics or tracking. The activity statistics shown in the app are counters stored locally and are never sent anywhere.

Updates and licenses

To check for updates, Hopper downloads a small file from GitHub, which receives a standard web request (such as your IP address).

If you activate a Pro license, Hopper sends the license key and a name for your Mac to Lemon Squeezy to validate it, about once a day.

Once a day at most, Hopper also downloads a small public file from hopperdev.app with the limits of the free version. It sends nothing about you or your connections.

Purchases

When you buy a license, Lemon Squeezy processes your name, email, billing details and payment as merchant of record, under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email and order details to provide the license and support.

This website

This site does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receive standard web requests.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you by writing to hopper@nexe.studio.
